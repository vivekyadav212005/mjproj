Meridian Corp — Shipping Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. Domestic Shipping
Standard shipping within the continental United States takes 5–7 business days. Expedited shipping (2–3 business days) is available for an additional fee of $12.99. Next-day delivery is offered in select metropolitan areas for $24.99.

2. International Shipping
International orders are shipped via tracked courier and typically arrive within 10–15 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the recipient. Meridian Corp is not liable for delays caused by customs inspection.

3. Free Shipping Threshold
Orders exceeding $75.00 qualify for free standard domestic shipping. This threshold applies to the merchandise subtotal before taxes and after discounts.

4. Order Tracking
A tracking number is emailed within 24 hours of shipment. Customers can monitor delivery status through the Customer Portal or the carrier's website.

5. Damaged or Lost Shipments
If a package arrives damaged, contact Support within 48 hours with photographs of the damage. Lost packages are investigated after 10 business days beyond the estimated delivery window. Replacement or refund is provided at Meridian Corp's discretion.

6. P.O. Box and APO/FPO Addresses
We ship to P.O. Boxes via USPS only. APO/FPO addresses are supported for standard shipping; expedited options are not available for military addresses.
