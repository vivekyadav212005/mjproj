Meridian Corp — Refund Policy (Updated Addendum)
Effective Date: March 15, 2025

1. Eligibility
Customers may request a full refund within 30 calendar days of the original purchase date. The product must be unused and returned in its original packaging. Digital subscriptions may be refunded within 14 days if fewer than 50 API calls have been made.

2. Process
To initiate a refund, submit a request through the Customer Portal at portal.meridiancorp.example or contact Support at support@meridiancorp.example. Include your order number and the reason for the return.

3. Refund Timeline
Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days.

4. Exceptions
Custom-manufactured hardware and promotional bundles marked "final sale" are not eligible for refund.

5. Restocking Fee
A 15% restocking fee applies to physical products returned after the original seal has been broken.
