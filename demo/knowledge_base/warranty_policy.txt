Meridian Corp — Warranty Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. Standard Warranty
All Meridian hardware products carry a 24-month limited warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use conditions.

2. What Is Covered
Manufacturing defects, component failure during normal operation, and firmware issues that prevent the device from functioning as documented are covered.

3. What Is Not Covered
Damage resulting from misuse, unauthorized modification, exposure to liquids, electrical surges, or use with non-approved accessories is excluded. Cosmetic wear such as scratches and dents is not covered.

4. Warranty Claim Process
Submit a warranty claim through the Customer Portal. Include your serial number, purchase receipt, and a description of the issue. Our technicians will respond within 3 business days with a diagnosis and next steps.

5. Repair or Replacement
At Meridian Corp's sole discretion, defective products will be repaired or replaced with a new or refurbished unit of equal or greater specification. Replacement units carry the remainder of the original warranty period.

6. Extended Warranty
Customers may purchase Meridian Shield, an extended warranty program adding 12 or 24 months of coverage. Meridian Shield must be purchased within 30 days of the original product purchase.
